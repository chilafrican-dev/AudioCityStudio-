# BeatPilot Full Soundza Library (0.12.8 source)

- Added all 142 distinct WAV recordings from the uploaded Soundza/LABADI Afrobeats Drum and Percussion One-Shots ZIP to `assets/samples/builtin/soundza`.
- Retains original 29 sample-bank entries, original 24 sampled drum sounds, and all five previously integrated sampled pianos.
- The Sound Desk sample browser now allows category filters: All, Loops, Drums, Percussion, Bass, Melody, FX.
- Individual file credit/license details are shown; do not relabel every source CC0.
- Source audio converted to 48kHz 16-bit stereo for the built-in Android WAV decoder.
- The Addictive sample pack and standalone Addictive loops are deliberately **excluded**.
- These are browser-accessible raw samples, **not** all assigned to percussion pads yet; assigning multisampled instruments and tempo-synced loops requires further implementation.
- Automatic pitch-preserving tempo stretching is **not** implemented in this release; current sample insertion can resample and alter pitch.
- This is Android source, not an installable or device-tested APK.
- Review bundled SOUNDZA-AFROBEATS.txt and original pack terms before public redistribution.
