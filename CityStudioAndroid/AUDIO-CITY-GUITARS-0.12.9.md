# Audio City Guitars — instrument pack integration

Includes three compact sample-based mobile SFZ/WAV conversions from user-provided FreePats SF2 instruments:
- Audio City Clean Guitar
- Audio City Direct Guitar
- Audio City Jazz Guitar

The names shown in the app are Audio City preset labels, not claims of ownership of the original audio recordings. Source README and LICENSE are preserved inside every factory ZIP.

All five existing built-in pianos remain installed, now with Audio City display names. The sample-browser filenames remain unchanged and full source/creator details remain in the credits.

These compact exports use 22.05 kHz mono samples with 12 sample roots / 2 velocity layers each. The full original SF2 quality is not reproduced. Verify on-device audio, memory and recording performance before release.
