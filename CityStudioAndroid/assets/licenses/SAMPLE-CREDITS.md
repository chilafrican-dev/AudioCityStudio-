## Freesound loops
The two source pages display Creative Commons 0 and permit copying, modification and redistribution, including commercially. The bundled versions derive from publicly offered high-quality MP3 previews; they are not lossless originals. The 99 BPM Nova source was divided into four four-bar excerpts.

## Processing
Trimmed, levelled, resampled to 48 kHz, and encoded as 16-bit stereo WAV. Loop lengths are aligned to their stated BPM and bar count. Very short edge fades remove clicks.

## FreePats FM Electric Piano 2 — bundled factory instrument

Sound samples and sound bank by Roberto for the FreePats project, recorded from the
open-source Hexter synthesizer. Bright FM electric piano inspired by the Yamaha DX7.
Original project: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/ElectricPiano/synthesized-piano.html#FM_Piano2

The APK bundles the original 12 WAV samples, the SFZ key mapping, README, and the
complete `LICENSE.txt`, all retained inside `assets/instruments/freepats-fm-piano2.zip`.
They are distributed under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. License:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
This is an electric piano, not a recorded acoustic grand piano.

## FreePats acoustic piano collection — built-in sampled instruments

**YDP Grand Piano — Compact Mobile Edition** (CC BY 3.0): This pack adapts
FreePats / Roberto's `YDP-GrandPiano-20160804.sf2`, built from the Zenph Studios
Yamaha Disklavier Pro multisamples originally prepared for OLPC by Dr. Mikhail
Krishtal and the Zenph Studios team. It retains five velocity layers and note
mappings, but the samples are shortened to at most 2.2 seconds with sustain loops
for Android memory limits. Attribution to those creators is required. Original
license text is included inside `freepats-ydp-grand-compact.zip` as
`LICENSE-ATTRIBUTION.txt` (Creative Commons Attribution 3.0).
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

**Kawai Upright Piano KW** — Full 2022 Edition, Lite 2019 Edition and Bright 2019
Edition (CC0 1.0). Source recordings of the Kawai upright piano made by
Gonzalo and Roberto for FreePats in January 2017. The lightweight FLAC formats
were converted to WAV without changing the instrument's SFZ key mapping, and
the loop indices are adjusted for engine resampling at load time. The original
`cc0.txt` and `readme.txt` are preserved in each bundled pack.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These are four distinct factory *choices*, not nine independent instruments:
the uploaded SF2, SFZ/WAV and SFZ/FLAC variants largely duplicate recordings.
The full SF2 and FLAC archives are not included as separate factory entries.
