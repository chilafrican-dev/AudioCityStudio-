# Audio City Studio 0.12.7 — Afrobeats drum kit integration

## Included
- 24 selected 24-bit WAV one-shots embedded in assets/instruments/soundza-afrobeats-drums.zip.
- SFZ instrument mapping MIDI notes 36–59 for playing in Play It. Keep the Take.
- Installer runs once on launch and the kit appears in the sampled instrument chooser.
- BeatPilot Make My Beat uses real sampled kick, snare and hi-hat from this kit when installed; the original synthesized pattern remains a fallback.
- Project arrangements remain non-destructive and include drum/bass/chord audio tracks as before.

## Limitations
- The full pack has 142 sounds; only 24 one-shots are embedded. No sample-loop tempo stretching or 16-pad beat sequencer was added in this version.
- Drum grooves currently use three selected sampled layers; additional per-kit sample choices need their own UI.
- Full Android SDK build and device tests have not run in this environment.
